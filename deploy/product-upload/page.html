How to prepare your package
  1. Unzip the starter and fill the Products sheet in its Excel template. Keep one row per colour and size.
  2. For each colour, enter an image_sku, for example KUR_001. Put its photos inside photos/KUR_001/. Use a different folder and image_sku for each colour.
  3. Keep camera filenames, such as DSC00137.jpg. Leave photo_names blank to include every photo in that folder, in filename order. Optionally enter exact filenames separated by ; to choose their order.
  4. For new or empty galleries, enter the preferred filename in main_photo; otherwise the first photo becomes the cover. Leave it blank for existing galleries to retain their cover.
  5. Select the Excel file and photos folder, compress them together into one ZIP, then choose that ZIP above.

Numbered photos such as KUR_001_1.jpg also work directly inside the ZIP. RAW camera files such as ARW must be exported to JPEG first. Review shows original photo paths before saving.

How photo matching works

Give each product colour a unique image_sku, for example KUR_001_RED. Name its photos KUR_001_RED_1.jpeg, KUR_001_RED_2.jpeg, and so on. Leave photo_names blank to match these numbered files. Alternatively, keep an image_sku and enter exact filenames in photo_names, separated by ;. New colours or empty galleries use the first photo as the cover. To choose a different cover for these galleries, use KUR_001_RED_2_main.jpeg or enter its filename in main_photo. When adding photos to an existing gallery, leave main_photo blank and omit _main; its current cover is kept. Use the same photo choices for every size of that colour. Filenames must be unique within the batch.

Save drafts or publish

Save as draft keeps products off the storefront. Publish uploads and confirms photos before making each completed product visible. Products are processed one at a time; a failed product stays in draft.

Updating a published product first moves it to draft, temporarily removing it from the storefront. Save as draft leaves it hidden; Publish to storefront publishes it again only after its details and photos are confirmed.

This tool does not add or change stock quantities. Use Receive stock after creating your products. Existing stock is retained. Open the existing import tools for other formats.
