1. Upload your products

Use one product per worksheet, repeated Product Name blocks, or one product per row. Up to 500 products and 100 visible worksheets. Embedded photos are skipped; add them after saving through SKN photo upload.

The template contains headings only. For rows, action is UPDATE or NEW. Blank details keep current values. Use skn, product_id, product_slug or hidi_sku to identify an existing product. Leave skn blank for new drafts. Set ananyas_pick to TRUE or FALSE; leave it blank to keep the current choice.

Optional common values for new drafts

These fill missing values only. Review each new draft before saving.

2. Review the batch

Expand a product to edit its details or untick fields. Existing prices, variants, stock, photos and publishing status are retained. New drafts start with zero stock. Casual Wear, Work Wear and Occasional Wear map to their website collections. Ananya's Pick can include products from all three; other collection choices are retained.

3. Save selected products

Nothing is saved until you click Save batch or Save selected products. Each product is saved separately; failures remain visible and can be retried. Completed products are excluded from retries. Missing MRP needs your review; it is not calculated from the selling price.

Upload SKN photos

Use the confirmed SKN shown after saving, for example 12345_001_main.webp or 12345_001_main.jpeg. M, L, XL and XXL of the same design share one SKN; existing size-specific SKUs remain available.
