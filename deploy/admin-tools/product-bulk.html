Sign in with your HIDI staff account

1. Upload your products

Use one product per worksheet, repeated Product Name blocks, or one product per row. Up to 500 products and 100 visible worksheets. Embedded photos are skipped; add them through bulk photo upload.

The template contains headings only. For rows, action is UPDATE or NEW. Blank details keep current values. Supplier style codes are descriptive; use product_id, product_slug or hidi_sku to identify an existing HIDI product.

Optional common values for new drafts

These fill missing values only. Review each new draft before saving.

2. Review the batch

Expand a product to edit its details or untick fields. Existing prices, variants, stock, photos, collections and publishing status are retained. New drafts start with zero stock. Category names must match an existing category or be chosen below.

3. Save selected products

Nothing is saved until you click Save batch or Save selected products. Each product is saved separately; failures remain visible and can be retried. Completed products are excluded from retries.
