## Measurement method

Use finished garments from the identified batch. Lay each garment flat without stretching unless the row explicitly calls for an extended waistband. Record millimetres (mm). Bust, waist and hip below are full circumferences: record a flat width separately in the evidence and double it for the circumference entry. Shoulder, sleeve and length entries are linear measurements and must not be doubled.

Record how the garment was positioned, where each measurement starts and ends, and any differences between samples or batches. Do not infer dimensions from size labels, photographs, another brand's chart or grading formulas. Do not assume equal increments between sizes. Obtain the supplier's tolerance before approving differences. Finished-garment measurements must not be described as body measurements.
