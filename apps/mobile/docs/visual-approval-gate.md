# Visual approval gate

The previous build reached functional depth without achieving the required retail quality. V2 therefore pauses after the four critical screens are implemented.

Approval package must contain:
- Home, Catalog/Filters, PDP and Bag screenshots on iOS and Android.
- 320, 360, 390 and 430dp widths.
- Light mode, 200% text and one offline/error state.
- Real HIDI product images and API-shaped data.
- A short interaction recording for wishlist, size sheet, filter sheet and bag swipe.

A screen fails the gate when it has any of these:
- Generic dashboard or admin-card appearance.
- Excessive empty space or oversized typography.
- Inconsistent gutters, radii or image ratios.
- More than one competing primary CTA.
- Pink accent used as a page background or decoration.
- Truncated product price/size/checkout information.
- Placeholder success for payment, inventory, loyalty or discount.

No full app expansion begins until the owner approves this package.
